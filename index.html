FAQ

Where can I listen?

Every chapter is on Spotify, Apple Podcasts and YouTube. You can also paste our RSS feed into any podcast app.

When do new chapters come out?

Every Friday at midnight. Where else would a noir story live?

Do I need to start at Chapter 1?

Yes. It’s a serial, so each chapter picks up where the last one ended. Every chapter opens with a short recap if you need a reminder.

Where does the name come from?

The title, Dan Flashes and Sammy Paradise are a tribute to Tim Robinson’s I Think You Should Leave. Our crooner, his cab and the story are original.

What’s on Elijah’s cab window?

Decals of a top hat and a cigar. Pull up exactly level with his cab and Elijah looks dressed for the stage. From any other angle, they look like what they are: stickers on a window. He knows. He’s working on it.

Is it based on a true story?

No. Vine Street is real, but Elijah, the Silver Slipper and everyone in it are made up.

Is it violent?

It’s a crime story, so there are gunshots and a body or two, but nothing graphic. Each chapter starts with a short content note.

How do you make the sound effects?

Mostly by hand. Footsteps on gravel, a cab door, rain on a windshield: we record it ourselves and credit any library sounds in the chapter notes.

Is there a transcript?

Better: every chapter’s full script is posted, so you can read along or catch a line you missed.
